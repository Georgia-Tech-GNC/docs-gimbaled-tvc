Verification Plan:



Complete a full integration cycle dry run, starting from all components of the rocket de-integrated, to the rocket on the (simulated) launch pad. 



The integration cycle should include all expected on-site testing, calibration, and configuration. All members who are expected to be at launch, or a qualified representative, should be present at the dry run.



Once the rocket is fully integrated and reaches the pad, the team should simulate the length it takes to set up the rocket on the launch pad, walk to a safe distance, and then wait for RSO approval.



Complete the dry run at least twice, logging the time it takes for each run.
