        TVC-REQ-001
        Ease of Integration

        The vehicle shall be fully integrated, launched, and recovered within 2 hours under nominal conditions.

        This requirement decomposes TVC-MO-004, specifically focusing on the vehicle, and giving a specific timeframe for complete turnaround. Most launch sites are open from 10 AM to 4 PM, so 2 hours is the absolute maximum each launch may take. This does not take into account failures, which are varied and more specifically specified in the Concept of Operations documents.

        TVC-REQ-014
        Max Dry Rocket Mass

        The max dry mass of the rocket (mass without motors) shall be a maximum of 1050 grams.

        A simple, 1D simulation was put together in order to Validate this requirement. That simulation can be found in docs/Configuration/TVC-REQ-013 folder. This simple 1-D simulation is required, as OpenRocket cannot properly simulate the full flight of an unstable rocket. The mass of the rocket in the simulation was adjusted to find the absolute maximum mass that allowed for around 50 feet of altitude gain, with some tolerance. This ensures the rocket, assuming a mostly vertical flight, will not reach the ground before motor burnout.

        Specific Controls Shutoff Conditions

        The rocket controls shall be turned off when unsafe conditions are reached.         TVC-REQ-023
        Control Loop Overall Architecture

        Every state in the state machine shall have clearly defined entry and exit criteria. Implementation of the entry and exit criteria shall match the criteri and prevent any invalid state transitions (Ex: Transitions to other states when unintended)

        Each state in a state machine must have a way to enter and exit. It is flight critical that the rocket does not enter a state when it is not intended for it to do so. For example, if the rocket were to enter the state pertaining to recovery prematurely, and prematurely deploy the parachute before motor burnout, that would be detrimental to the mission objectives.

        TVC-REQ-024
        Motor Burn Time

        The motor shall have a burn time of at least three seconds.

        In order to demonstratably show the rocket controlling its attitude, it must have a long enough burn time to give the control system time to correct any errors in atttitude. Assuming it takes the rocket ~1 second to clear the launch rail, a minimum of a three second burn time gives the rocket ~2 seconds to correct any errors in attitude. From previous program experience, this should be enough to discernebly notice whether the control system is reacting to the other, along with collecting enough data to do so.

        TVC-REQ-038
        Launch State Transition

        The flight software shall detect launch, and once it does transition the state machine to powered ascent (Where active controls will be active). The conditions to detect launch shall be: Acceleration above +0.5G for 50 ms.

        According to OpenRocket, the thrust:weight ratio off the pad is ~2. Removing gravity, this is 1G acceleration. Applying a scaling factor of 0.5, which balances sensitivty and gives a tolerance for acceleration, this gives a launch condition of +0.5G. 50ms ensures that random disturbances do not trigger the launch (Control loop runs at >20Hz), while also ensuring that launch is detected quickly, reducing time when the rocket does not control itself.
