    TVC-MO-002
    2. Respond to Environmental Factors
    UID: TVC-MO-002
    Record type: Objective
    Subteam owner: Systems
    Individual owner: Systems Lead
    Vehicle configuration: TVC-F1
    Mission phase: Powered Ascent
    Flight attempt: Flight 1
    Open item status: None
    Status: Draft
    Statement:

    Demonstrate that the vehicle can maintain attitude in response to reasonable environmental factors, such as wind gusts. Other environmental factors include temperature, humidity, and pressure.
    Rationale:

    This mission objective demonstrates the program goal of thrust-vector control, even in response to expected environmental factors. Reasonable is based on the average expected conditions at the launch site. For example, a reasonable wind condition would be the average maximum gust over the course of a week.

    TVC-MO-008
    9. Controls Shutoff Condition
    UID: TVC-MO-008
    Record type: Objective
    Subteam owner: Controls
    Individual owner: Controls Lead
    Vehicle configuration: TVC-F1
    Mission phase: All
    Flight attempt: All
    Open item status: None
    Status: Draft
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • TVC-REQ-018 Specific Controls Shutoff Conditions
    Statement:

    Demonstrate that the vehicle can shut off controls when determined that the rocket attitude can not be recovered. Shutting off controls is defined as actuating the gimbal to its known 0 position, then cutting power to the gimbal actuators.
    Rationale:

    This mission objective ensures that the rocket does not try to control attitude at a point when it can no longer do so, and it may be unsafe to continue attempting to do so. Lower level requirement more clearly defines the conditions where rocket attitude can not be recovered.

    UID: TVC-MO-012
    Record type: Objective
    Subteam owner: Systems
    Individual owner: Systems Lead
    Vehicle configuration: TVC-F1
    Mission phase: Pre-Launch
    Flight attempt: All
    Open item status: None
    Status: Draft
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • TVC-REQ-040 Overcurrent/Reverse Polarity Protections
    • TVC-REQ-041 Labeling Wires Before Launch
    • TVC-REQ-042 Gimbal Replaceable Motor Case
    • TVC-REQ-043 No last Minute Changes
    Statement:

    TVC-MO-013
    16. Control Initial Delay
    UID: TVC-MO-013
    Record type: Objective
    Subteam owner: Software
    Individual owner: Software Lead
    Vehicle configuration: TVC-F1
    Mission phase: Pre-Launch
    Flight attempt: Flights 1-3
    Open item status: None
    Status: Draft
    RELATIONS (Child):
    • TVC-REQ-051 Controls algorithm delay
    Statement:

    Demonstrate that the vehicle can clear the launch rail before beginning to actuate the gimbal.
    Rationale:

    The rocket should not attempt to control itself until it has cleared the rail, as the rail is controlling the attitude at this point, and gimballing is likely to just cause more friction between the rail buttons and the rail.
